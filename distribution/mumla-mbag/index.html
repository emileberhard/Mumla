Install on Mac

  1. Open the DMG and drag Mumla into Applications.
  2. Launch it from Applications; Mumla lives in the menu bar.
  3. Follow the setup assistant for permissions and access to the transcription service.

Your dictation data

  • Speech is transcribed by Microsoft's MAI-Transcribe-2 through Mumla's access service, which stores no audio or text.
  • History, dictionary entries, and original audio sync through your private iCloud database.
  • Mumla never captures your screen. Crash diagnostics go to Sentry.
